CHITRAAR NIGHT WEB EXPERIENCE
==================================================

DIRECTORY STRUCTURE:
--------------------
Chitraar-Night/
│── index.html
│── style.css
│── README.txt
└── chitrahaar-night-background.jpg

HOW TO RUN LOCALLY:
-------------------
1. Place your background image file into this directory and ensure it is named:
   "chitrahaar-night-background.jpg"
2. Double click "index.html" to open it in any standard browser.

HOW TO DEPLOY TO VERCEL:
------------------------
1. Compress/Zip the entire "Chitraar-Night" folder into a single ZIP file.
2. Go to https://vercel.com/new
3. Import or drag & drop your folder/repository.
4. Click Deploy. Your site will be live on a custom .vercel.app domain!
